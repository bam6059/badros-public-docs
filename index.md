# BadrOS personal workspace

BadrOS is a privately operated personal workspace. Its owner runs the application
on their own computer and accesses it from paired desktop and mobile clients.
This public website contains documentation only. It does not provide access to
the private application, personal files or mailbox.

## Optional Gmail connection

The Gmail integration lets the owner connect their Google account, search and
read selected messages, save selected message text in their local workspace,
and send an email with a specified recipient and message. Google authorization
is required before accessing Gmail.

External AI processing of Gmail content is on hold until the selected provider's
applicable data-use and retention arrangements have been verified against
Google's Limited Use requirements. Google authorization alone does not approve
this processing. Connecting Gmail does not authorize sending the whole mailbox
to an AI provider.

BadrOS is a personal application, not a public email service or a Google product.
Gmail API access does not require a paid BadrOS subscription. Separately selected
AI services may charge for their use.
